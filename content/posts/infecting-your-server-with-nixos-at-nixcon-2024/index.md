## About the workshop

Installing NixOS on a server can be challenging for beginners or time-consuming when installing it on multiple machines. This workshop explored how to make the process simpler and which tools can help.

I wrote a [step-by-step guide to installing NixOS with nixos-infect](/posts/infecting-your-server-with-nixos/) to help prepare for and follow along with the workshop.
